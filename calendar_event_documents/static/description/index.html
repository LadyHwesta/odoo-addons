Meeting Documents

Attach documents to meetings and send them with invitations

What it does

A Documents tab on every meeting for the agenda, minutes and papers, shown on the calendar and sent with the invitation.

On the meeting

Add, download and remove files on a Documents tab; whoever can open the meeting can open them.

With invitations

Documents are attached to invitation and update emails (over 15 MB, the email says to open the meeting instead).

In the video call

They are pinned in the call's chat as download links, for guests too.

Private documents

Keep a file for your team only: not emailed and not shown in the call.

Setting it up

  1. Open a meeting in Calendar and use its Documents tab.
