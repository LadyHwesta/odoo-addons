Gig Bookings

Booking requests for performers: gig details, e-signed quotes with a deposit, no double bookings

What it does

For DJs, bands and other performers booked for events. A request form on your website creates a lead with the gig: date, times, venue, type of event and guests.

Quotes carry the same details, Odoo's own online signature and deposit book the date, and every date is checked against your other gigs and events first.

Booking request form

A ready Book a Date page on your website, protected like any other Odoo form.

Never booked twice

Gigs, appearances and events too close together are flagged, and confirming one is stopped until you say so.

Booked Dates calendar

Every confirmed gig and event in one place.

Balance reminders

A to-do to invoice what's left, a set number of days before each gig.

Setting it up

  1. Install it, and turn on Online Signature and Online Payment (with a deposit) in Sales settings.
  2. Link your website to the Book a Date page (/book).
