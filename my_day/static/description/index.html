My Day

A start screen to plan and work through your day, across your companies and branches

What it does

Today's calendar, your to-dos, and calls and texts to answer on one side; what needs attention on the other: threat status for security officers, tickets, leads, tasks coming due, money owed and due, quotes waiting on customers, this week's events.

Each section has its own colour, and everyone can pick a background picture or use their own.

It follows the company switcher, so with several companies or branches selected you see them all, each item marked by company. Make it the first screen after login.

Plan the day

Today's meetings with a Join button, to-dos you can tick off, voicemails and callbacks.

Needs attention

Helpdesk tickets, tasks due, money owed and due, quotes waiting, events.

Companies and branches

Money per company, a badge on every item, one tap to see one company.

Your start screen

One tick in Preferences opens it after login.

Only what you may see

Sections appear for installed apps and your access rights.

Setting it up

  1. Install it and open My Day.
  2. Tick Start on My Day in your Preferences to open it after login.
