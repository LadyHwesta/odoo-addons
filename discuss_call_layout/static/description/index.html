Video Call Gallery View

Gallery and Speaker view for Discuss video calls, like Zoom and Teams

What it does

Discuss video calls get the two views people know from other video apps: Gallery, with everyone tiled, and Speaker, where whoever is talking fills the screen.

One button

Switch views from the call's controls; each person's choice is remembered on their device.

Back after screen sharing

When a screen share ends, the call returns to the chosen view instead of staying on the presenter.

For guests too

Works for people who join from a meeting invitation link.

Setting it up

  1. Install it, and start or join a video call: the Gallery / Speaker button is in the call controls.
