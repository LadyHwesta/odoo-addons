Event Appearances

List events you're performing at: link to the host's tickets, show their flyer

What it does

Performers, speakers and vendors appear at events someone else runs. Listing those as normal events makes Odoo try to sell tickets and take registrations for them.

Mark an event as an appearance and it is listed on your website with your own events, but nothing is sold here: the page shows who is hosting, their flyer and materials, and a button to where people buy tickets or register.

Link to the host

Get tickets, Register or More info, opening the host's page; or Free entry.

Their flyer and materials

Images and files from the host, on the event page.

A badge in your events list

Shown on each appearance, e.g. "Performing".

Nothing sold by mistake

Odoo's registration and tickets are switched off for appearances, even if someone posts to them directly.

Setting it up

  1. Install it, open an event and tick Appearance.
  2. Choose how people get in and paste the host's link.
