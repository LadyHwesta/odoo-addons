Gig Song Requests

Guests request songs from a QR code; clients set must-play and do-not-play lists

What it does

Every booked gig gets a private song request page and a QR card to stand on the tables. Guests request a song from their phone, and the page closes when the gig ends.

Before the gig, your client fills in must-play and do-not-play lists from their customer portal. You see everything on the gig, and requests on the do-not-play list are flagged.

QR card for the tables

Print it from the gig; it opens the request page on your own website.

Protected and rate-limited

Turnstile or reCAPTCHA if you use them, a hidden bot trap, and a limit per phone.

Client music lists

Must play (with the moment, e.g. First dance) and do not play, from the customer portal until the gig starts.

Songs to Get

Mark each song Got it, or Can't get with a reason the client sees.

Played or skipped

One tap per request during the gig.

Setting it up

  1. Install it (it needs Gig Bookings).
  2. On a booked gig, open the Music tab and use Print Song Card.
