App Folders and Order

Put apps in your own order and group them into folders

What it does

Odoo lists apps in its own order, and module upgrades reset any order you set by hand.

This module lets you drag every app into the order you want and group them into named folders, and keeps that order through upgrades.

Your order

Drag apps into the order your team uses them; apps outside a folder come first.

Folders

Name folders such as "Money", "Phones" or "Events" and drag them into order.

Kept through upgrades

The order lives in its own records, so upgrading modules doesn't undo it.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Settings, Users & Companies, App Drawer.
  2. Drag apps and folders into order, and save.
