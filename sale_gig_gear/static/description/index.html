Gig Gear Checklist

Your gear list, default gear per package, and a packing checklist on every gig

What it does

Keep a list of the gear you take to gigs: speakers, lights, mics, cables, and how many of each you own. On each package or add-on, say which gear it needs.

Every booked gig then gets its own packing checklist, and the load-in sheet prints it with boxes to tick as it goes in the van.

Checklist on every gig

Filled from the booked packages when the gig is confirmed; add or change items by hand.

Packed ticks

"5 of 12 packed" at a glance, and a printed list to tick on the day.

More than you own

A gig needing more of something than you have is flagged.

Load-in sheet

Times, venue, on-site contact, notes and gear, on one page.

Setting it up

  1. Install it (it needs Gig Bookings) and add your gear in Sales > Configuration > Gear.
  2. Open each package product and list its gear on the Gig Gear tab.
