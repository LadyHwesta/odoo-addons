Invoice Payment Reminders

Automatic reminder emails for unpaid customer invoices

What it does

A simple reminder schedule for customer invoices: a few named stages, each a number of days before or after the due date, with its own email.

Every day, each unpaid invoice gets whichever stage is due, once per stage.

Ready to use

Four stages out of the box, from a friendly note before the due date to a final notice.

Your wording

Each stage has its own email template you can edit.

Once per stage

An invoice never gets the same reminder twice, and paid invoices get none.

Setting it up

  1. Install it; the four stages are created for you.
  2. Adjust the days and wording of each stage in the Accounting settings if you like.
