Assign Tasks to Contacts

Make a contact responsible for a task or subtask

What it does

Odoo's task assignees are internal users only. This module adds a Contact Assignee to tasks and subtasks, so a client, volunteer or partner can be the one responsible.

Any contact

Pick any contact as the task's assignee, separate from its customer.

Kept in the loop

The contact follows the task, so its chatter emails reach them.

Tasks and subtasks

Works the same on both.

Setting it up

  1. Open a task and choose its Contact Assignee.
